# Privacy Notice — OX Platform

_Last updated: 2026-06-28. This is the product privacy notice template; tenants
white-labeling OX substitute their own controller details._

## Who we are
OX (the "Platform") is the data processor; the partner floor / brand operating a
tenant is the data controller for its members' data. Contact:
**privacy@ox.fit** · DPO: **dpo@ox.fit**.

## What we collect
- **Account:** name, email/phone, role, home floor.
- **Training:** workout sessions, sets, PRs, recovery, body metrics (sensitive —
  explicit consent).
- **Commerce:** orders, tickets, memberships (card data is tokenized by Stripe;
  OX stores only Stripe ids).
- **Usage:** device, locale, and essential cookies; analytics only with consent.

## Why (lawful bases — GDPR Art. 6)
- Contract — to provide the service you signed up for.
- Consent — analytics, marketing, health-app sync (withdrawable anytime).
- Legitimate interests — security, fraud prevention, product improvement.
- Legal obligation — tax, accounting.

## Your rights
Access, rectification, **erasure**, portability, restriction, objection, and the
right to withdraw consent. Exercise them in **You → Settings → Privacy** or by
emailing privacy@ox.fit; we respond within 30 days (GDPR) / 45 days (CCPA).
California residents: we do **not** sell personal information; GPC opt-out signals
are honored.

## Sharing
Stripe (payments), hosting/cloud (infrastructure), and the tenant floor you train
with. We do not sell personal data. Sub-processors are listed at /legal/subprocessors.

## Retention
Per data class; training/biometric data is deleted on account erasure, which
cascades through related records.

## International transfers
Where data leaves your region, transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses /
adequacy decisions. Regional hosting is available to tenants.

## Children
Not directed at users under the local digital-consent age (13–16 in the EU);
gated at signup.

## Changes
Material changes are notified in-app and by email.
